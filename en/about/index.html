About

feminine • effortless • bold — shoes that finish the look.

Our story

LUNA is two stores in Batumi under one brand. In May 2026 our store at 7 Merab Kostava St in Old Town became a shoe store: women's footwear, beachwear and accessories.

Clothing and jewellery moved to our second LUNA store in Batumi View. So you can build a whole look: the dress or jacket there, the pair and the bag with us.

feminine • effortless • bold

How we choose

We choose every pair ourselves — beautiful, comfortable and current. We always state the material: natural suede, natural leather or faux leather. New pairs arrive in small batches, so sizes are limited.

The space

Light tiles, lit white shelves, greenery and a cosy chair for trying on. Come and try in person — we'll help with sizing and match a pair to your look.
